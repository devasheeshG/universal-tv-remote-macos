# Universal TV Remote for Mac 1.0.2

Fixes repeated Fire TV entries and stale connection status, with automatic recovery when a remote service becomes available.

## Fixes

- Merge changing Fire TV Bonjour service identifiers and DIAL discoveries into one remembered device.
- Clean existing duplicates while preserving pairing credentials, nicknames, and the selected TV.
- Restore an already paired TV after delayed discovery and reconnect when its address changes.

## Connection status

- Monitor Fire TV and Roku using read-only requests and recover when their remote services return.
- Show Reconnect only while disconnected or after a connection failure.
- Remove automatic Fire TV wake commands from connection and background monitoring.

## Install

Requires **macOS 14 or later**. Download `Universal.TV.Remote.for.Mac.dmg`,
open it, and drag **Universal TV Remote.app** onto **Applications**.
On first launch, **right-click → Open** and allow local network access when
macOS asks. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.

## Compatibility

“Connected” describes remote-service availability, not the power state of
an attached display. A powered Fire TV Stick can remain reachable while
its television is off. Available commands and app queries vary by firmware.
Samsung, LG, and Roku drivers have not been verified on physical hardware by
the developer.

## Distribution update

This historical release now provides a DMG containing its original app
